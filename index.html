Meditation #2

by Maceo

For this meditation I drew inspiration from an app I use called Moises. I originally downloaded it to find chords for lesser known songs that I enjoyed that I wanted to play on guitar. However, it quickly became apparent that the AI model was not as accurate as I would have liked. Still, the app had other benefits including a metronome to adjust playback speed, a reverser, and my favorite tool, a sound isolator for vocals, drums, bass, and melody. I enjoy removing vocals from many of my favorite songs, just listening to the beat, and experimenting with it using the metronome and reverse options.

For the meditation, this is exactly what I planned to recreate. I made my playback buttons first for speeding up and slowing down and then created a reverse button. When I looked into ways to remove vocals, I realized it would require more advanced coding or APIs. Because of this, I decided to look into the visual side of things and wanted to make the background change with the average frequency of the song. Lower frequencies produce warmer colors with red, orange, and yellow, while higher ones bring darker colors with green, blue, and purple. I found this was fun to play around with especially with the 'speed up' and 'slow down' buttons.
